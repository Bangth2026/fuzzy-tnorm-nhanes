# Pre-specified test on NHANES August 2021–August 2023 (cycle L)

Written before any cycle-L data file was downloaded, cleaned or analysed by this project. The commit that adds this
file fixes the plan; the commit that adds the cycle-L checksums and results comes after it. Only the public codebooks
(variable names and response codes) were read to write `nhanes_l.py`.

## Why this cycle
Both targets used so far (2007–2012 and 2005–2006) had been inspected before path-consistent completion (PCC) and the
blends were designed. Cycle L has not been opened. It is the first NHANES cycle after 2017–2018 with the PHQ-9 that
does not overlap the development cycles (the 2017–March 2020 pre-pandemic files contain 2017–2018).

## Sample and concepts
Adults with all nine PHQ-9 items valid and a positive MEC weight (`WTMEC2YR`). Concepts and membership functions are
those of `clean2.py`, with the changes forced by the cycle-L questionnaires and coded in `nhanes_l.py`:
marital status from `DMDMARTZ`; self-rated health from `HUQ010` (no general-health item in `HSQ_L`); inactivity from
leisure-time activity frequency (`PAD810Q` vigorous, `PAD790Q` moderate). In cycle L the PHQ-9 was self-administered
(ACASI) in the mobile examination center. Strata and PSUs: `SDMVSTRA`, `SDMVPSU`.

## Fixed before download
Development relation: 2013–2018, unchanged. Nothing is tuned on cycle L.
- PCC: lambda = 0.003, gamma = 0.5, the minimax-regret choice over random-dyad and type-block folds on 2013–2018 with
  the extended lambda grid {0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3} (`results2/pcc2_dev.json`, `choices.two`); in the
  type-block design, the setting chosen without the evaluated block (`block_choices_two`).
- Rank blend: w * rank(product path) + (1 - w) * rank(logit soft-impute), ranks over the cells withheld by one mask
  (over all 552 ordered pairs in the single-dyad design), w = 0.5 chosen by the same minimax rule on 2013–2018; per-block
  weights from `block_choices_two`. The relation-scale blend (w = 0.8) and the raw-scale blend (w = 0.6) are secondary.
- Other methods as in the paper: product best path of two or three edges, Katz, logit soft-impute with the penalty
  chosen by dyad cross-validation within development, rank-3 completion.
- Designs: single dyads withheld (552 ordered pairs, Spearman rho) and whole type blocks withheld (pair-weighted mean of
  within-block rho). Regret of a method in a design: gap to the best of six methods (PCC, product, Katz, logit
  soft-impute, rank-3 completion, rank blend). Worst-case regret: the larger of its two regrets.
- Intervals: JRR over the strata of 2013–2018 and of cycle L (one PSU deleted per stratum, the other doubled), two-sided
  95%; TOST on the 90% interval with margins 0.03 and 0.05 where stated.
- Code: `python3 pcc_checks.py c21` (results2/pcc2_c21.json); `python3 pcc_checks.py c21regimes` for the descriptive
  regimes.

## Hypotheses and decision rules
P1 (hedging against the product). Worst-case regret of the product minus that of the rank blend > 0.
   Met if the 95% interval lies above 0.
P2 (does the path term add to a blend?). Worst-case regret of PCC minus that of the rank blend > 0.
   Met if the 95% interval lies above 0.
P3 (replication of the PCC claim). Worst-case regret of the product minus that of PCC > 0.
   Met if the 95% interval lies above 0. If not met, TOST with margin 0.03 is reported.
S1 (RQ1). Single dyads: rank-3 completion minus product > 0 (95% interval above 0).
S2 (RQ2). Symmetric relation (276 dyads): product minus Hamacher; met if the 95% interval contains 0; TOST at 0.03 and
   0.05 reported.
S3 (minimum rule). Directed relation: product minus minimum > 0 (95% interval above 0).
D1 (descriptive). Random 25% and 50% of dyads, cold start with 1, 3 and 6 anchors, two simulated-source designs: rho of
   every method (point estimates, means over repetitions) and the worst-case regret over all regimes run on cycle L.

All results are reported whatever their direction. If cycle-L files cannot be mapped as above (for example a
variable is absent), the change is reported and the test is labelled as deviating from this plan.
